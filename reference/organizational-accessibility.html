European Agency model: Policy (strategy, statement, procurement policy), Plan (realistic, with authority and resources), Practice (pilot, training, style guides, processes, testing before release).

Levels 2 and 3 swap names. BDF = I-D-R-M-B; CMM = I-R-D-M-O.

  • Champions: role models from key areas; build vision, sustain commitment, support programmatic integration, lead maturity model adoption
  • Evaluation: early and often, born accessible, human evaluation critical, include people with disabilities
  • Hiring: inclusion statement, disability job boards, accessible postings; skills incl. WCAG 2.1, PDF/UA
  • PR: consult legal or risk and compliance first
  • Procurement: verify claims and expertise, contract it, review roadmaps, use policy to influence vendors
