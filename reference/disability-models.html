Disability models

Drill target: strengths and weaknesses. Source: BoK pp. 4–7. Grows with each lesson. All seven models.

ModelTellStrengthWeakness
Medicalcure, treat, fixAddresses biological causesIgnores inaccessible environments and attitudes
Socialbarriers, exclusionShows barriers are not inevitableCan downplay embodied aspects
Charitypity, donationsMobilizes real helpCondescending; short-term focus
Economicwork, productivity, benefitsRecognizes real support needsLegal "needy" category stigmatizes
BiopsychosocialBody+mind+society; rehab; ICFBridges medical and socialICF classification is complex
Functional solutionsIdentify limit, engineer around itResults oriented, practicalProfit-driven tech misses; ignores social fixes
Social identityGroup pride; Deaf cultureAccepts disability as prideOutsiders feel excluded

Relationships: charity ← medical + economic. Biopsychosocial = medical + social. ICF (WHO, 2002) ← biopsychosocial (Engel, 1977). Functional solutions = the accessibility pro lens.

Economic vs charity weakness: economic = legal "needy" label and eligibility threshold; charity = pity, condescension, short-term fixes.

Acronyms

Charity is related to medical AND economic (not social identity). Biopsychosocial is the attempt to blend medical + social. Steps example: medical blames the person, social blames the steps.
