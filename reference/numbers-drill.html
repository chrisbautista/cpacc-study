Count ladder (people)

  • 200M intellectual disability
  • 246M low vision
  • 284M anxiety disorders
  • 430M disabling hearing loss
  • 2.2B any vision impairment

Smaller counts: 19M bipolar, 24M schizophrenia (1 in 300), 50M epilepsy. Hook: bipolar 19M < schizophrenia 24M, and "300" belongs only to schizophrenia (autism is 1 in 100).

Percentages and ratios

  • Dyslexia 5 to 10% (up to 17%); 70 to 80% of people with reading difficulties (most of them: 7 or 8 in 10). The only range reaching 10.
  • Dyscalculia 3 to 6%. The narrowest.
  • ADHD children 2 to 7%, adults about 4%; 2 to 3 in 10 attention only (a minority: about a quarter). Kids 6 symptoms, adults 5.
  • Anxiety 2.5 to 7%. Starts at 2.5, ADHD starts at 2.
  • Autism 1 in 100; NLD about 1% of US children; schizophrenia 1 in 300.
  • Photosensitive epilepsy about 3%; flash rate 16 to 25 per second (3 to 60 extremes); 3 flashes per second limit.
  • Red-green color blindness 1 in 12 males (a dozen, 8.3%), 1 in 200 females (0.5%). Deaf-blind 0.2 to 2%. Auditory processing disorder about 5%. Speech sound disorders: children 5 to 25%, adults 1 to 2%.
