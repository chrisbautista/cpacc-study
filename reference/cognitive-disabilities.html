Cognitive disabilities

Source: BoK pp. 20–26. Parts 1 to 3 so far; grows with later lessons. Index · Acronyms

Seven mental functions: attention, memory, psychomotor, emotional, perceptual, thought, calculation. External factors: information overload, stress, sleep deprivation. Strategies and AT are grouped by function, not diagnosis.

Mind map: ADHD, autism, NLD

Dyslexia vs dyscalculia hook: dyslexia 5-10% is about double dyscalculia 3-6%. Reading trouble is the more common of the two.

Prevalence trio: dyscalculia 3-6% is the narrowest range. ADHD (children) 2-7% is the only one starting at 2. Dyslexia 5-10% is the only one reaching 10, and the highest.

Count ladder: 200M (intellectual disability) < 246M (low vision) < 430M (hearing loss) < 2.2B (any vision impairment).

Web barriers, solutions and AT (BoK pp. 24-26)

Barriers: finding information, forms, passwords, understanding information, understanding/using controls, keeping focused, information overload, time-outs. Others meet them to a lesser degree; a person with cognitive disabilities may give up.

Solutions (G-P-I): General (time, feedback, structure, plain language), Physical (easy-to-find rooms, large plain signage, simple wayfinding), ICT (simplified distraction-free interfaces, text/audio/images, highlight key info, personalized settings).
