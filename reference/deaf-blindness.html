Deaf-Blindness

Source: BoK pp. 14–15. Index · Acronyms

TypeDefinitionPrevalence
Deaf-BlindnessBoth deafness and blindness. Most are not completely deaf nor blind. Touch is the only viable channel for complex communication. Braille for text; tactile sign language for conversation (feel the signing hands).0.2% to 2% of the world's population (World Federation of the Deaf-blind)

Barriers: no printed braille; incomplete braille output; no braille transcripts of video/audio; no tactile sign interpretation. Solutions/tech: braille transcripts, tactile sign interpretation, screen reader to refreshable braille device, printed braille, haptic alerts, cane, service animals, tactile navigation aids, Deaf-Blind communicator.
