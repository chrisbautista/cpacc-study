Seizure disabilities

Source: BoK p. 27. Lesson: 13.

ItemFact
SeizureSudden, uncontrolled electrical disturbance in the brain; mild to severe, may include loss of consciousness.
Epilepsy2 or more seizures, or a tendency to recurrent seizures. 50 million people (WHO).
Photosensitive epilepsySeizures triggered by flashing or flickering lights or patterns. About 3% of people with epilepsy. Two groups: pure photosensitivity, and also seizures at other times.
Flash rate16 to 25 per second most likely; range 3 to 60.
PatternsHigh contrast or moving; some video games.
Web limitNo more than 3 flashes in any one second, or below general and red flash thresholds.

Barriers

  • Sudden loss of consciousness in swimming, bathing, power tools
  • Moving, blinking or flickering content
  • No controls to stop video or animation

Assistive technology

  • Diary apps with reminders
  • Smart watches that detect seizures (alerts, GPS)
  • Service animals
  • Wearables with alert button
  • Protective wear
  • Flicker-free monitors, glare guards, non-glare glasses
