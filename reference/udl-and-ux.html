UX core areas (U-U-D-A-C):

  • Useful content
  • Desirable content

Usability: a sub-discipline of UX. Easy and proficient use, achieve goals, easy to learn.

User-centered design: user at the center of every stage; involve users throughout; iterate; include accessibility testing in the cycles.
