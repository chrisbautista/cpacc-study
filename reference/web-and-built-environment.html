4 principles, 13 guidelines (4-5-3-1). The outline says WCAG 2.1, the BoK text says 2.2; the counts are the same.

Web accessibility covers:

  • Auditory
  • Cognitive
  • Neurological
  • Physical
  • Speech
  • Visual

Also benefits (S-O-T-S-B):

  • Small screens and other inputs
  • Older people
  • Temporary disabilities
  • Situational limitations
  • Bandwidth limits

Built environment:

  • UD principles were originally developed for it
  • Cost of leaving UD out is often greater than construction cost
  • Minimum standards = compliance, often accommodation
  • Best practice guidelines = best result
  • Focus: access in and out (safety, evacuation), moving around, transport, policies
