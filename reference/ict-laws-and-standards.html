  • Air travel

  • Department of Transportation

  • TV and on-demand
  • Progressively more accessible
  • Accessible emergency information

  • Telecoms
  • Accessible contract summary

  • Emergency services access
eIDASEU
  • Electronic signatures and trust services
Procurement Directive 2014/24/EUEU
  • Accessibility in technical specifications
  • May weigh accessibility in evaluation
  • May exclude unreliable operators
Federal Acquisition RegulationUS
  • Implements Section 508
  • Comparable access for employees and public
Section 508 StandardsUS
  • Government sites
  • Based on WCAG 2.0
ADA Title IIIUS
  • Places of public accommodation, incl. e-commerce
  • No legal standard for private sites
  • DOJ Civil Rights Division enforces
  • Education OCR for schools
Web Accessibility Directive 2016/2102EU
  • Public sector sites and apps
  • From September 2019
  • Statement, reporting method, enforcement link
EN 301 549EU
  • Voluntary harmonized standard
  • Annex A presumes conformance to the WAD
  • WCAG 2.1 plus extras
European Accessibility ActEU
  • Private businesses, except microenterprises
  • From 2025
  • ATMs, smartphones, e-books, e-commerce and more
  • Member States enforce

Hooks (ours):

  • AVMSD: "AV" is audiovisual, so TV and on-demand
  • ADA enforcer: Justice (DOJ Civil Rights Division); schools: Education OCR
  • EU Charter Article 21 = non-discrimination; 26 = integration

EAA standards: revised EN 301 549 (ICT), EN 17210 (built environment), EN 17161 (Design for All), plus three new ones (non-ICT information, support services, emergency communications and 112).
