Universal design and benefits

Source: BoK pp. 36–38. Lesson: 18. Index · Acronyms · Map

TermMeaning
Universal designAs many people as possible can use it without adaptations. Does not prevent adaptive devices; can reduce the need.
Reasonable accommodationSpecific modification or adjustment for equal access in a specific case.
AccessibilityEquivalent user experience for people with disabilities, without barriers or discrimination.
UsabilityEase of use and user experience; not always considering disability.
Design for allUsed in Europe. EN 17161:2019 Design for All.

Related concepts (I-D-H-L):

  • Inclusive design
  • Design for all
  • Human-centered design
  • Life-span design

Benefits:

  • People: education, employment, social activities
  • Families and society: independence, contributions, cost savings, better built and digital world
  • Organizations: innovation, problem solving
  • Macro: global GDP loss from labor-market exclusion

Hook: A = Access for people with disabilities, U = Use is easy, U = Under no target group.
