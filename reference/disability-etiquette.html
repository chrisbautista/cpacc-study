Disability etiquette

Source: BoK p. 35. Lesson: 17. Map.

Guidelines for interacting with and communicating about people with disabilities in an inclusive, respectful way: behavior, attitudes, respectful language.

Five interaction rules (D-A-A-A-R)

  • Direct: speak to the person, not to the person accompanying them
  • Assume nothing about what they can or cannot do
  • Ask before helping: assist only on request or after asking permission (impact varies widely)
  • Acknowledge their ability to make their own decisions and judgments
  • Respect personal space and privacy: no touching a wheelchair or equipment without permission

Pairing hooks: UN (UNCRPD, plus G3ict and IAAP) = people first. Self-advocates = identity first: "self" goes with "identity".

Rule of thumb: for a specific person, ask what they prefer.
