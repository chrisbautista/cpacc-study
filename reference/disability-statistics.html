Disability statistics

Source: BoK pp. 33–34. Lesson: 16. Map.

Limitations

  • Census surveys usually lack details about different disabilities
  • Terminology varies between countries
  • Multiple disabilities fall between defined groups

Still useful as background and for the scale of an issue.

Uses (Eurostat, University of New Hampshire)

  • Public policy: health, social protection, housing, transport, culture, education, employment
  • How many people with disabilities per country, region, world
  • Percentages by type and age group
  • Employment, disposable income, poverty

Collisions: 1.3B all disability (WHO) < 2.2B any vision impairment. 16% (1 in 6) of the world; 10 to 15% of people with disabilities have complex needs.
