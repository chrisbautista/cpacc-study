National and provincial disability laws

Source: BoK pp. 55–58. Lesson: 23. Index · Acronyms · Map

LawYearKey point
US Rehabilitation Act, Section 5081973
  • Accessible electronic and information technology
  • Comparable access for the public
US ADA1990
  • Civil rights law
  • Public accommodations, employment, transportation, state and local government, telecommunications
EU Employment Equality Directive2000
  • Employment and occupation
  • Requires reasonable accommodation
  • Implemented by all Member States
Ontarians with Disabilities Act2001
  • Provincial (Ontario)
  • No new barriers, remove existing ones
UK Equality Act2010
  • England, Scotland, Wales
  • 9 protected characteristics, disability one
  • Direct, arising from disability, indirect

Reasonable accommodation: case by case; not required if a disproportionate burden. Accessibility: applies regardless of disability status.

Protected characteristics (9): age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, sexual orientation.

EU: 2008 general Anti-discrimination Directive proposed, not adopted. Health care laws fall into 4 groups: both, discrimination only, accommodation only, neither.
