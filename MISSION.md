# Mission: CPACC (Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies)

## Why
Pass the IAAP CPACC exam (scaled score 600+/800) within ~4 weeks as a career credential in accessibility.

## Success looks like
- Score 600+ on the exam: Domain One 40%, Domain Two 40%, Domain Three 20%
- Given a scenario, name the disability model, law category, or UD principle at play, and say why
- Recall definitions, numbers and distinctions from memory, not by recognition

## Constraints
- ~4 weeks to exam; short lessons, heavy retrieval practice
- Strong on tech/WCAG; weak on disability models and categories, etiquette, laws and treaties
- Source of truth: `references/CPACCBoK.pdf`. When notes and PDF conflict, the PDF wins.

## Out of scope
- Hands-on coding / WAS-level technical depth (that is a different exam)
