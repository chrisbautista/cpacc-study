Full mock exam

Win for today: a full-length rehearsal. 100 multiple-choice questions in 2 hours, weighted about like the exam (Domain 1 40, Domain 2 40, Domain 3 20). No feedback until you submit, no peeking at notes, and the clock runs. The questions are drawn from every lesson, with the answer positions evenly spread.

1. Explain it simply (Feynman technique)

Skip this today. Instead, set up the way you will sit the exam: close the notes and the palaces, silence your phone, and use a clear desk. Answer every question, flag nothing, and keep moving at about 72 seconds per question. If you are unsure, pick your best answer and go on.

3. After you submit

  • Paste the summary line, the by-domain line and the missed list. The missed list shows the source lesson (for example L22) next to each miss.
  • Spend no more than 45 minutes on review: re-read only the missed items' lessons or sheets.
  • The pass mark is a scaled 600 of 800, not a percentage, but a raw 75% or more here is a comfortable sign.
