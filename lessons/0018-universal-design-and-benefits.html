CPACC · Domain Two (40%) · 2A and 2B

Universal design, accommodations and benefits

Win for today: tell universal design from an individual accommodation, separate accessibility, usability and universal design, and name who benefits from accessibility. This is Domain Two A and B. Follows disability etiquette.

1. Explain it simply (Feynman technique)

A ramp built when one guest asks is an accommodation: a fix for one person. A step-free entrance designed for everyone is universal design: no fix needed. Universal design shrinks the number of fixes but never bans them. Accessibility is about people with disabilities getting an equal experience. Usability is about ease of use for anyone. Universal design names no target group at all. When people with disabilities take part, families, employers and the whole economy gain, including GDP.

2. The idea

Source: BoK Domain Two A and B, pp. 36–38.

Universal design vs accommodation:

  • Universal design: a product or service developed so as many people as possible can use it without needing adaptations
  • Reasonable accommodation: a specific modification or adjustment, in a specific case, to give a person with a disability equal access and opportunity
  • Universal design does not prevent adaptive devices or accommodation where needed. It can reduce how much they are needed.

Related concepts (same common idea: design so as many people as possible can use it without adaptations):

  • Inclusive design
  • Design for all: used particularly in Europe. EN 17161:2019 Design for All helps organisations develop accessible products, goods and services.
  • Human-centered design
  • Life-span design

Three concepts that emphasise different things:

ConceptEmphasis
AccessibilityPeople with disabilities get an equivalent user experience, without barriers or discrimination
UsabilityEase of use and user experience; does not always consider the needs of people with disabilities
Universal designInclude everyone to the greatest extent possible, without specifying any target group

Benefits of accessibility (2B):

  • People: take part in education, employment and social activities needed for health and happiness
  • Families and society: more independence, varied contributions, cost savings, a better built and digital world for everyone
  • Organizations (schools, employers, groups): more innovation and better problem solving, from diversity through inclusion
  • Macro level: evidence of a global loss of GDP from excluding people with disabilities from the labor market

5. Mnemonics (ours, not the BoK's)

  • Related concepts: "I-D-H-L". Inclusive, Design for all, Human-centered, Life-span.
  • Accessibility, usability, universal: A = Access for people with disabilities, U = Use is easy, U = Under no target group (everyone).
  • Accommodation = one: one person, one case. Universal = many.
  • Benefits ladder: person, family and society, organization, world economy (GDP).
