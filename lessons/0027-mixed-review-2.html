3. After the quiz: hooks for the usual slips

  • Charity model: related to medical and economic, not social identity.
  • Defined vs Repeatable: BDF is I-D-R-M-B; CMM is I-R-D-M-O.
  • EU Charter: Article 21 = non-discrimination (blackjack, everyone gets the same fair deal); Article 26 = integration (blend into one smoothie). CRPD Article 9 = accessibility.
  • Framework counts: UDL has 3 guidelines (why, what, how); WCAG has 4 principles; UD has 7 principles.
  • AVMSD: "AV" is audiovisual, so TV and on-demand.
  • ADA enforcer: Justice (DOJ Civil Rights Division).
  • EU Web Accessibility Directive vs European Accessibility Act: public sector 2019 vs private business 2025.
